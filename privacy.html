Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2025

At Streameko, your privacy is as important as the next episode of your favorite anime. Our philosophy is simple: we don’t want your data. The site is built from scratch to not collect, store, or share any personal information. That’s it.

🚩 "If something is free, you are the product"

Yeah, you’ve heard that one. But not here. Streameko is free because we love anime as much as you do — not because we’re mining your data. No trackers, no shady algorithms. Just a clean, fan-made website for fans.

1. The Information We Don’t Collect

Let’s be brutally honest: Streameko’s website collects absolutely nothing. We’re not interested in:

  • Your name, email, or any contact info (except in the cases mentioned in section 2).
  • Your location.
  • Your watch habits, favorite anime, or history.
  • Any telemetry or usage analytics.

When you use Streameko, you’re basically a ghost. A happy ninja watching anime. Enjoy your freedom.

2. The Little Information We Could Have

There are only two specific cases where we might access your data — and usually, it’s because you provided it voluntarily.

A. If You Contact Us or Submit a Form

In most contact cases, providing your email address is optional. If you don’t enter it, we’ll never see it. If you email us directly (soporte@streameko.qzz.io) or use a form that requires it, obviously we’ll see your address and whatever you include in your message. We’ll use it only for one purpose: to reply.

IP Address Collection for Security: When you submit any form (like contact or DMCA claims), our system automatically collects the IP address used for the submission. This info is collected only for service and user security reasons, to prevent abuse, spam, fraud, or malicious attacks. We do not use this IP to track your browsing habits or physical location (and we only have it when you send a form).

B. If You File a DMCA Claim

The law’s the law. If you’re a copyright holder and submit a DMCA (Digital Millennium Copyright Act) claim, we’ll need certain info to process it legally, such as:

  • Your full name.
  • Your email address.
  • Proof that you own the rights or represent the owner.

This information is strictly confidential. It’s only used to manage your claim and contact you about it. It stays safe in our inbox, away from prying eyes.

3. Data Use, Storage, and Retention

The (very little) info you provide is stored securely in our email provider or server logs. We’ll never share it with third parties, advertisers, or any external entity, unless required by a valid court order.

Data Retention and Annual Deletion Policy

We actively delete all stored data at the start of each calendar year — including emails and IPs — unless there’s a legitimate reason to keep it.

  • Users currently banned or restricted due to abuse.
  • Legal requirements or valid court orders.
  • DMCA claimants (their data is retained indefinitely unless they explicitly request deletion and the claim is withdrawn, rejected, or resolved).
  • Users involved in legal action against Streameko (data is retained until the case concludes).
  • Users who specifically requested longer or shorter data retention (we’ll follow that request).

We seriously dislike storing data, so if there’s no legal or justified reason to keep it, it’s gone by the start of each year.

4. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy (unlikely, since our policy is “have no data”), we’ll update it right here. Check back occasionally — just in case.

5. Questions?

If you’ve got questions about this policy — or just want to chat about why Bocchi is the best character ever — feel free to contact us.
